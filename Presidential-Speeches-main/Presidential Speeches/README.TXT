Our and people were used the most in modern times. Government was the most used during the middle era and united was used the most during the early era likely due to the recent unification of the United States. 
Early political speeches prioritized defining institutional mechanisms, constitutional framework, and federal structure. Modern rhetoric shifted toward personal engagement, inclusivity, and shared national identity, leading to a dramatic increase in collective pronouns like our and direct appeals to the people.
